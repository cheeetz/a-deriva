SHORT DESCRIPTION
A fisherman lives on your computer's taskbar. He fishes on his own while you work, from the
Araguaia to the sea off Noronha. You glance over now and then, click when one bites, and buy a
bigger boat. It also runs as a wallpaper.

You're free to use the screenshots, GIFs, video and art in this kit in articles, videos and
streams about À Deriva.
