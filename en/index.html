A river at the bottom
of your screen

There is a strip of your screen nobody uses. That thin band just above the taskbar, between one spreadsheet and the next.

We put a river there. A little boat, a fisherman in a straw hat and a line in the water. And if the strip feels too small, the river takes the whole monitor: it runs across your wallpaper, behind your desktop icons.

While you work, he fishes. When you look again, something has changed: it rained, night fell, a river dolphin went by. You don't have to play. The hard part is not looking.

Made for those who love to drift

It fishes for you

Fish bite in their own time. If you are there, a click at the right moment makes a difference. If you are not, that is fine — the river holds no grudge against someone who went to a meeting.

From the Araguaia to Noronha

It starts on a river of sandbanks and sun in your face. Then come the black water of the Rio Negro, the hills of Furnas, the Santa Catarina coast — and, at the end of the line, a sea where what bites can be bigger than your boat.

The weather is in charge

Rain arrives slowly. The storm rocks the boat. And there are nights when the moon turns red — and then not even the river knows what is going to bite.

Company along the way

A caiman eyeing the hook. A giant otter that only picks the fat ones. A river dolphin nudging the school your way. They show up when they please — and when they do, they stay.

The river changes its mood

The hat you catch is yours. For real.

Every fisherman starts with the same straw hat. Not for long.

At some point, without warning, a stork crosses your screen carrying a sack. It does not stop and it does not wait. You click — and what falls out of the sack does not stay stuck inside the game. It goes straight to your Steam inventory.

From then on, the hat is yours. To wear on your fisherman, trade with a friend or sell on the Steam Market to someone still waiting for their own stork.

Everyone already has the straw hat. The rest, only the stork brings.

Every hat has a life

Not every hat arrives the same way. Some come battered, from too much river. Others come worn, the brim already shaped to somebody's head. And a few — very few — arrive pristine, as if they had never seen water.

To Steam, they are different items. Each one with its own value on the Market.

Now picture a pristine Imperial Crown. Legendary is already the top of the draw, and pristine is the hardest condition to get. It is the kind of piece few people will ever own — and whoever owns one will know exactly what they are holding.

And no, this is not pay-to-win. A hat gives a little nudge, never a shortcut — the best of them helps less than the laziest animal on the boat. A hat is meant to be rare, beautiful and yours. The edge comes from time with your line in the water.

The Wardrobe

Hats live in the Wardrobe, and that is where the collection takes shape. What you own, you wear. What you are still missing shows up as ???, in the rarity's color and nothing else.

You know it exists. You just do not know what it is.

And selling does not erase history: a hat that passed through your hands stays marked there, even after it is gone.

Two hats no stork carries

The Jester's Hat and the Boi-bumbá do not fall from the sky, do not come in any sack and are not sold in our shop. They do what no other hat does: the bait that is never used up, the fish that comes in pairs.

There is only one road to them: the monthly championship. The winner takes the celestial in pristine condition. Second place takes the worn one, third takes the battered one. The first edition is for the Jester's Hat.

If you ever see one of these on the Market, you can be sure of one thing: somebody won.

Every fish has its own face

None of them was painted a different color to pass for another fish. The riverbank mandi, the flooded-forest piranha, the open-sea marlin — each one has its own drawing.

And every place keeps a monster that will not rise to just any bait. On the Araguaia, they say it is a hundred-year-old piraíba.

From canoe to open sea

Everyone starts in a canoe. One day the hold gets too small and the canoe becomes a dinghy. The dinghy becomes a speedboat. And before you know it, you are out on the open sea, in a trawler that makes the canoe look like a toy.

And the river has other residents

A caiman on the bank, a jaguar lying on the sand, a snake at the water's edge, a frog on a giant lily pad, a macaw crossing the sky, a pelican skimming the sea. They are worth no points, give no bonus and ask for no clicks — they pass by because rivers have animals.

And every so often a cat on a life ring drifts past, or a rubber duck. Those two we cannot explain.

Where this river came from

À Deriva was not born in a big studio. It was born in Brazil, made by one person, with a stubborn idea: you could have a river running in the corner of your screen without getting in anyone's way.

The places came from home. The Araguaia, the Rio Negro, Furnas, the Santa Catarina coast, the sea at Noronha — each with its own fish, its own bait and the animals we have known since we were kids. The boi-bumbá became a hat. The pororoca became weather. The jabiru got a spot on the boat.

Before it got here, the game went through the hands of people who played it, complained and made suggestions — and a lot changed because of them. Now the river is heading to Steam.

What is coming not yet

Pirate's Pack

Every river has a story nobody can confirm.

This one begins on a moonless night. A dark hull slips through the fog without a sound. No one on deck, no one at the helm — just torn sails, full of a wind that is not blowing, and a parrot in a bandana squawking from the mast at nothing.

It is the Flying Dutchman. Haunted, and not the least bit sorry.

The captain vanished long ago. The parrot did not. He is out looking for a new boat to call home — and he brought along the only thing left of his old master: the pirate tricorne, whole, just as it came out of the chest. You can find a tricorne on these rivers, worn out by the water. Pristine, only this one.

The Pirate's Pack is the founder's edition: the ghost ship, the parrot and the pristine tricorne. For anyone who wants to be part of this story from day one — and keep the proof in their inventory.
