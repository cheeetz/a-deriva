Donate

I am going to be straight with you

There is no campaign, no goal, no little bar filling up. There is a guy who makes a fishing game in the hours that are left over, and some bills to pay for it to exist outside my projects folder.

What this money does

US$ 100 to open the Steam door. That is the fixed price of publishing there, and it has no discount and no sale.

Around US$ 120 a year for the code signing certificate. That is what stops Windows from saying the game came from an "unknown publisher". Right now whoever downloads it gets that scare before opening — and some of them close it there and never come back.

After that it is time. Fish to draw, maps to finish, sound to record, the swamp I keep promising and have not delivered.

There is no office, no team, no publisher. There is me, an open Godot and a notebook full of things to do.

itch.io Coming soon. For card payments, from anywhere.
Pix, any amount Brazilian instant transfer, straight from your bank. If you are not in Brazil this one will not work for you — the itch.io page above is the one to wait for.

What you get

Nothing inside the game. No item, no shortcut, no currency, no advantage — people who donate and people who do not catch the same fish, in the same river, at the same speed.

That is not modesty, it is a decision. The day donating becomes an advantage, the game becomes something else, and that something else you can already play in ten other places.

What you get is outside the game: you backed it. When this is on Steam, with a page, a trailer and reviews written by people I have never met, part of it will have come out of your pocket. And I will know exactly who was here when it was just a folder with an .exe in it.

Cannot help with money? Good, there is something better

I am going to say something that sounds like a man who does not want to sell you anything: your report is worth more than your money.

I open this game every day. I no longer see what is wrong with it — I am past the point where that was possible. You open it for the first time and see all of it at once: where it stalled, where it got boring, where you had no idea what that button did.

That has already changed the game several times. And it cost me nothing.

If you are reporting something broken, send the logs folder with it — the home page says where it lives.
